      Sources

      Every source carries a trust tier, which decides how its items may be used. Health comes from the last sync.
      Verified
      Official government, CERT or standards body. Actionable.
      Trusted
      Established vendor or community source with review. Corroborate before blocking at scale.
      Unverified
      Claims without independent confirmation, such as leak-site listings. Monitor only.
      Untrusted
      Failed validation or unknown provenance. Excluded from reports.

      Settings

      The console runs offline from its saved dataset. Live sync needs the local companion (python3 serve.py), which runs the collector and returns the feed. Keys stay on that machine.
