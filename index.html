Source Verification

Every feed carries a trust tier. Tier decides how an item may be used in reporting.

Verified
Official government, CERT or standards body. Actionable.
Trusted
Established vendor or community with editorial review. Corroborate before blocking at scale.
Unverified
Claims with no independent confirmation, such as forum posts or aggregator listings.
Untrusted
Failed validation, unknown provenance or known-false reporting. Excluded from reports.
